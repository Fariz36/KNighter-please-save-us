## Bug Pattern

A `container_insert` path consumes/frees the inserted object without first calling `container_remove` on it. An object that may still be linked in its previous parent/list is passed to a `deallocator`, leaving the container with a dangling reference to freed storage. Later traversal or destruction of the container can cause use-after-free/double-free.

Roles:
- `container_insert`: `xmlAddChild`
- `container_remove`: `xmlUnlinkNodeInternal`
- `deallocator`: `xmlFreeNode`
