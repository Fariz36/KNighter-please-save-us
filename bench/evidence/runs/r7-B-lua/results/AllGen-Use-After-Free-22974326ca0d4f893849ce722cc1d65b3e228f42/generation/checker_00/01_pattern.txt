## Bug Pattern

A GC-managed table object (`h`) is used across a potentially GC-triggering operation (`luaH_finishset`) without being anchored on the Lua stack. When `h` is obtained through an `__newindex` metamethod chain and is only weakly referenced—e.g. it is a value in a weak metatable—an emergency collection during `luaH_finishset` can collect and free the table while it is still being updated, causing a use-after-free. The fix anchors the table by pushing it onto the lua stack before the call and popping it afterward.
