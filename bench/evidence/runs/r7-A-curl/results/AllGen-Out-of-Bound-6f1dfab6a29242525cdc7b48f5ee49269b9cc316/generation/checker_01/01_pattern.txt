## Bug Pattern

Parsing a delimiter-terminated string by advancing past a found delimiter (`ptr++`) and then unconditionally reading fixed later offsets (`ptr[1]`, `ptr[2]`, `ptr[3]`) without first checking that the current character (`ptr[0]`) is non-NUL / that enough input remains. If the string ends immediately after the delimiter, `ptr[0]` is the NUL terminator and the subsequent `ptr[1]` access reads one byte past the terminator. The fix adds `sep &&` to short-circuit before any out-of-bounds access.
