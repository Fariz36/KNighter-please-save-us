## Bug Pattern

The result of a `duplicator` / `null_on_failure` call is passed to a `length_of` operation before being checked for NULL. If the duplication/allocation fails and returns NULL, the `length_of` call dereferences NULL. The NULL check and cleanup happen too late.

Roles:
- `duplicator` / `null_on_failure`: `xmlStrdup`
- `length_of`: `strlen`
- `allocator`: `xmlMalloc`
- `deallocator`: `xmlFree`
