## Bug Pattern

Local automatic variables are declared without an initializer and can be read on paths where they were never assigned. In `cli_opt_help_fprint`, `required` is used on the right-hand side of its own assignment before initialization when handling `CLI_OPT_USAGE_CHOICE`. In `git_diff_driver_load`, `drv` can reach the `done:` cleanup label and be tested/freed before it has been assigned, such as after an early failure. The fix initializes such variables (`required = true`, `drv = NULL`) so they always have a defined value before any possible use.
