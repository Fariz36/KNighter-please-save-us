## Bug Pattern

A locally owned resource (`pFromDup`) is conditionally transferred into `pTriggerStep->pSrc`, but there is no cleanup on the path where the transfer does not occur. If `pTriggerStep->pSrc` is `NULL`—for example after an earlier allocation failure—the append is skipped and the allocated/duplicated `SrcList` is leaked. The root cause is missing deallocation on the non-ownership-transfer/error path.
