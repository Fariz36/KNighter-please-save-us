## Bug Pattern

An untrusted or externally configurable value (`maxAmpl`) is accepted without validating that it is non-zero, then later used as a divisor (`*expandedSize / ctxt->maxAmpl`) without a zero guard. This allows the divisor to become zero, causing a division-by-zero fault (SIGFPE). The fix rejects zero in the setter and adds a `> 0` check before the division.
