## Bug Pattern

A `cursor` is advanced past a delimiter found by a `substring_finder`, then fixed-offset `lookahead_read`s are performed on the cursor before checking that the cursor’s current byte is not the `string_terminator`. If the delimiter is the final character, the current byte is `'\0'`, and the first lookahead reads one byte past the NUL terminator. The missing guard is a `non_terminator_guard` on the current byte before any lookahead.

Roles in this patch:
- `substring_finder`: `strchr`
- `delimiter`: `'('`
- `cursor_after_delimiter`: `ptr` after `ptr++`
- `current_byte`: `sep` / `ptr[0]`
- `string_terminator`: `'\0'`
- `lookahead_read`: `ptr[1]`, `ptr[2]`, `ptr[3]`
- `classification`: `ISDIGIT(ptr[3])`
- missing `non_terminator_guard`: the added `sep && ...` check
