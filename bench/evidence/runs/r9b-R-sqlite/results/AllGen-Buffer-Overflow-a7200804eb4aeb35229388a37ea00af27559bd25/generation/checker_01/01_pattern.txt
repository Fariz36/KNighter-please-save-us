## Bug Pattern

A buffer-capacity calculation is passed to a buffer-enlarging `reallocator`, but the calculation underestimates the number of bytes that the subsequent conversion path will write. In particular, it omits conditional fixed characters emitted by the alternate-form path and the terminating NUL. The `reallocator` therefore reserves too little space, and the later direct stores / `buffer_copy` / `memset` writes go past the end of the buffer, causing an out-of-bounds write.

Roles:
- `reallocator`: `sqlite3StrAccumEnlargeIfNeeded`
- `buffer_copy`: `memcpy`, `memset`
