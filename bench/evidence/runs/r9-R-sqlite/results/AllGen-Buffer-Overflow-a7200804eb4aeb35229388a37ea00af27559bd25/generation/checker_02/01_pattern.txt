## Bug Pattern

The required output-buffer size passed to a capacity-reserving `reallocator` is computed from variable field lengths plus a fixed overhead, but that fixed overhead is too small for all literal characters that the subsequent formatting code can write (sign, decimal point, exponent marker/sign/digits, and terminator). The `reallocator` therefore reserves too little space, and later `buffer_copy`/store operations write past the reserved destination.

Roles:
- `reallocator`: `sqlite3StrAccumEnlargeIfNeeded`
- `buffer_copy`: `memcpy`, `memset` (and the direct stores into the accumulator buffer after the reservation)
